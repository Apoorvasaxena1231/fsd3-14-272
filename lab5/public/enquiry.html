Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bluetooth?

Bluetooth is a wireless technology used to connect nearby devices without cables.

What devices support Bluetooth?

Many devices such as headphones, speakers, watches, keyboards, mice and gaming controllers support Bluetooth.

Is Bluetooth easy to use?

Yes. Most Bluetooth devices can be connected through a simple pairing process.
